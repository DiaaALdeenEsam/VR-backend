What this data is

This is a clinical-training VR simulator database. A scenario describes a patient case (in Arabic) with a hidden gold standard diagnosis. A trainee opens a session against a scenario, chats with a simulated patient (messages), orders lab/imaging tests (grouped into test categories, recorded as ordered_tests), and answers multiple-choice questions built from choices, with each attempt logged in answers.
